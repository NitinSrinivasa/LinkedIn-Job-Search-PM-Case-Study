# LinkedIn Job Search Optimization

A product experimentation case study exploring how LinkedIn could help students and recent graduates discover internship and new-grad opportunities earlier in the recruiting cycle.

The case study evaluates three approaches:
- verified community-submitted job discovery
- partnerships with specialized job-discovery platforms
- improvements to LinkedIn's internal search and sorting experience

I prioritize a verified community-submission model and define a proposed experiment, success metrics, trust-and-safety guardrails, risks, and launch/iterate/stop criteria.
